Pacific Pain Clinic and Dr. Cyrus Sedaghat respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information provided through the website, appointment requests, and clinic interactions.

1. Information we collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, medical history, and other details you choose to provide. Website usage data may include device, browser, IP address, and pages visited. Health information may be collected when voluntarily submitted to support care or respond to inquiries.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to manage appointments and medical care, respond to inquiries, communicate updates, improve services and patient experience, and meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

3. Sharing information

Pacific Pain Clinic does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers supporting clinic operations, healthcare professionals involved in care, or when required by law or needed to protect rights, safety, or property.

4. Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies and analytics tools to understand visitor behavior and improve performance. Browser settings can be adjusted to refuse cookies, although some website features may then work differently.

5. Data security

Administrative, technical, and physical safeguards are used to help protect personal and health information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

6. Your rights

You may have the right to access copies of personal data, request corrections, or request deletion of certain information, subject to legal and medical record-keeping obligations.

7. Third-party links

The website may link to external sites. Pacific Pain Clinic is not responsible for their privacy practices, and visitors should review the policies of those websites.

8. Policy updates

This policy may be updated as practices, services, or regulations change. The latest version will remain available on this page.
