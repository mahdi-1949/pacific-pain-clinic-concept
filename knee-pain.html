A PRECISE APPROACH

Knee pain is not one condition.

The knee is a complex, load-bearing joint. Effective care depends on identifying whether pain is degenerative, traumatic, inflammatory, or biomechanical.

Dr. Sedaghat evaluates the joint, surrounding soft tissues, movement patterns, and relevant imaging before recommending treatment. The goal is not simply to mask pain, but to preserve function and help you move with more confidence.

KNEE PAIN FAQ

Answers before your first visit.

Individual recommendations are made after an evaluation.

Do I need a referral?

In many cases, no referral is needed. Some insurance plans may require one, so check with your provider.

What should I bring?

Bring a photo ID, insurance card, medication list, and relevant medical records or imaging.

How long is an initial appointment?

Initial consultations commonly last 30–45 minutes, depending on your needs.

Is surgery always necessary?

No. Pacific Pain Clinic focuses on advanced non-surgical and joint-preserving options when clinically appropriate.
