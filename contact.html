BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Common questions.

Do I need a referral?

In most cases, no referral is needed. Some insurance plans may require one, so check with your provider.

What should I bring?

Bring your photo ID, insurance card, medication list, and relevant medical records or imaging.

How long is the first visit?

Initial consultations commonly last 30–45 minutes; follow-up visits are often shorter.

What conditions are treated?

Dr. Sedaghat specializes in back, neck, joint, nerve, and chronic pain management.
